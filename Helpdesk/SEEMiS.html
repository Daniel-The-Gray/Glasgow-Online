Help and FAQs
How do I reset my SEEMiS password?
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the SEEMiS login page, or raise a ticket using the SEEMiS Access category.
How do I book training?
Select Training Request as your category and describe the module or topic you need support with.
Can I update a staff record myself?
Some changes require administrator approval. Use Staff Record Update to submit a change request.
